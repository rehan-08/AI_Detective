During the Monday condition check I found that the Lisbon Codex in the Vault is a forgery. The binding is close, but the vellum fluoresces under ultraviolet.

The vault door log shows a single opening on the evening of Friday 10 October, during the donor dinner. I was at home in Ealing from 18:00 until 23:30 that evening.

The trustees had agreed to explore a private sale of the Codex. Dr Brandt opposed the sale in writing and Dr Marsh led the negotiations.
