I sat at the front table in the Ballroom all evening. Mr Hale's address was over well before the auction. I remember checking my watch when he sat down and thinking how early it was, not yet half past ten.

Mr Hale was very attentive and asked me twice at the start of the evening whether I had kept the necklace in my room.
